About Vlasis

Vlasis Ryzhevich is a Cypriot filmmaker and animator based in Rotterdam, NL.

Read more

Vlasis works with 3D and 2D techniques, constantly experimenting with new methods and mixed media to develop a visual language that feels his own. This curiosity is driven by the themes he returns to again and again: identity, and the struggle to know who you are, both as an artist and as a person. He wants to share stories that have the power to inspire people forward, help them identify a problem they've been carrying around in silence, or simply remind them that they're not alone.

He's currently studying animation at the Willem de Kooning Academy, where he's continuing to develop his skills in 3D. He's open to internships, work opportunities, and collaborations.
